An Educational Experiment

The project explores how a technology-enabled learning environment can make learning more engaging while continuously measuring progress.

Track → Measure → Change

The long-term vision is to move beyond simply providing questions and answers. The platform is intended to help understand how students are learning, identify areas of difficulty and support more targeted intervention.

The initial focus is on Science, Mathematics, General Knowledge and other subjects suitable for school students in Grade VI–X.

Our two central objectives Track, measure and improve learning — while creating high-quality, engaging and useful educational content.

Key App Features

Designed around student simplicity, teacher participation and measurable learning progress.

📝

Interactive Quizzes

Students can access default quizzes as well as quizzes created or assigned by teachers and administrators. Each attempt is tracked separately.

⏱️

Timed Practice

A quiz timer starts automatically. The current configuration supports up to 20 minutes for 20 questions, while the system remains adaptable.

🔁

Multiple Attempts

Students can retry quizzes and build a history of participation, scores and improvement between attempts.

🔗

Teacher Connection

Students can request to join a teacher. Once approved, the teacher can support associated students and share educational content.

📚

Learning Materials

The platform can provide books, PDFs and teacher-assigned educational material alongside quizzes and other learning resources.

🔐

Email OTP Login

Authentication currently uses an email One-Time Password, avoiding the need for users to create and remember a traditional password.

Who Can Participate?

The project is community-driven. Different participants can contribute in different ways as the platform develops.

🎓 Students

Learners can practise, participate in quizzes and provide real-world feedback.

  • Grade VI–X learning
  • Quiz participation
  • Progress and attempt history

👩‍🏫 Teachers

  • Review participation and performance

  • Developers & AI/ML contributors

Privacy, Safety & Account Control

Privacy-conscious development is an important principle of the project.

Data Privacy Statement

User data is intended to be used for app functions and learning progress. The project does not intend to share user data with third parties for commercial, advertising or unrelated purposes.

Users can request deletion of their data if they wish to leave the project or have their information removed.
